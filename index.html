How the proposed pilot would work

An administrative service that records and reminds. Decisions about insurance stay with you and your broker.

  1. Forward certificates when the pilot opens

    Send the certificates your subs give you to one dedicated inbox. Nothing new for your subs to learn.

  2. Record what is printed

    Insured, insurers and NAIC numbers, policy numbers, dates, printed limits and the holder, exactly as printed. Anything unclear is held for a person to check.

  3. Prepare reminder drafts for your review

    Before a printed expiry date, a draft asks the sub for an updated certificate, worded the way you approve. Nothing goes out without your approval.

A free tracker you can use now

What the proposed pilot would not do

  • No coverage advice

    We don't interpret policies, endorsements or contract terms, or tell you whether a policy is enough. A certificate is a summary; the policy itself governs.

  • No broker recommendations

    We don't recommend brokers, carriers or products, and we aren't paid by any of them.

  • No pass/fail judgements

    We show your entered requirements next to the printed values. Deciding what that means, and whether a sub can work, is up to you.
