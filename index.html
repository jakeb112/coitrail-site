How it works

An administrative service: we record and remind. Decisions about insurance stay with you and your advisers.

  1. Forward the certificates (once the pilot opens)

    Send the COIs your subs give you to a dedicated inbox. No new software for your subs to learn.

  2. We log what's printed

    Insured, insurers and NAIC numbers, policy numbers, effective and expiry dates, printed limits and the certificate holder. Fields the software can't read confidently are flagged and held back, never shown as final.

  3. Track dates and send reminders

    See printed expiry dates at a glance, view your own entered requirements beside the printed values, and approve the reminder wording sent to subs ahead of renewal.

What we don't do

COI Trail records what a certificate prints and keeps track of dates. It is not insurance advice.

No coverage advice

We don't interpret policies, endorsements or contract terms, or tell you whether a policy is enough. A certificate is a summary; the policy itself governs.

No broker recommendations

We don't recommend brokers, carriers or products, and we aren't paid by any of them.

No pass/fail judgements

We show your entered requirements next to the printed values. Deciding what that means, and whether a sub can work, is up to you.
